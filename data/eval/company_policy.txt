Acme Corporation — Employee Handbook (Revised January 2025)

1. Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of continuous service. New employees
who join mid-year receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their start date.

Unused annual leave may be carried over into the following year, up to a maximum of
5 days. Any carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year or they
will be forfeited. Employees may not receive cash compensation in lieu of unused
annual leave except upon termination of employment.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance for periods of 3 days or fewer, and at least 15 business days in advance for
periods exceeding 3 days. Managers must respond to leave requests within 2 business
days.

2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not
accrue and resets on January 1 each year. Unused sick leave is not carried over and
is not compensable upon termination.

A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive business days.
Employees must notify their direct manager by 9:00 AM on the first day of absence. If
the employee is unable to make direct contact, they must send an email to both their
manager and the HR department.

3. Remote Work Policy

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to
manager approval. Remote work arrangements must be documented using the Remote Work
Agreement form available on the HR portal.

Employees working remotely must be available during core business hours (10:00 AM to
4:00 PM local time) and must maintain a reliable internet connection with a minimum
speed of 25 Mbps download and 5 Mbps upload. Remote workers are expected to attend
all scheduled meetings via video with their camera enabled.

The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for employees who work
remotely at least 2 days per week. This stipend is intended to cover ergonomic
furniture, monitors, or other equipment. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days
of purchase.

4. Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense management system within 30
days of being incurred. All claims must include itemized receipts. Submissions without
proper receipts will be rejected.

The following daily spending limits apply for business travel:
- Meals: $75 per day (domestic), $100 per day (international)
- Ground transportation: $50 per day
- Accommodation: up to $200 per night (domestic), up to $350 per night (international)

Air travel must be booked in economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 6 hours,
in which case premium economy is permitted. Business class requires prior written
approval from a Vice President or above.

Reimbursements are processed within 15 business days of approved submission and are
included in the next available payroll cycle.

5. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to act with integrity, professionalism, and respect in all
business interactions. The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
- Misuse or unauthorized disclosure of confidential information
- Accepting gifts or entertainment valued at more than $100 from vendors or clients
  without prior approval from the Compliance department
- Use of company resources for personal commercial activities
- Falsification of records, timesheets, or expense reports

Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including
termination of employment. Employees are encouraged to report potential violations
through the anonymous Ethics Hotline or directly to the Compliance department.

6. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually in June and December. Each review
cycle includes a self-assessment, manager evaluation, and a one-on-one feedback
meeting. Performance is rated on a 5-point scale:

1 - Does Not Meet Expectations
2 - Partially Meets Expectations
3 - Meets Expectations
4 - Exceeds Expectations
5 - Significantly Exceeds Expectations

Employees who receive a rating of 1 or 2 are placed on a Performance Improvement
Plan (PIP) lasting 90 days. Employees who do not demonstrate satisfactory improvement
during the PIP period may face reassignment or termination.

Annual merit increases are tied to performance ratings and are typically awarded in
February, effective retroactively to January 1.

7. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must
commence within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

Employees on parental leave continue to accrue annual leave and are entitled to all
benefits, including health insurance and retirement contributions. Upon return,
employees are guaranteed the same or an equivalent position.

8. Training and Professional Development

Acme Corporation allocates an annual training budget of $2,000 per employee for
professional development activities including conferences, courses, certifications,
and books. Requests must be approved by the employee's manager and submitted to HR
at least 30 days before the activity.

Employees who leave the company within 12 months of receiving training reimbursement
exceeding $1,000 are required to repay the full amount. This repayment clause is
waived in cases of involuntary termination not related to performance or misconduct.
